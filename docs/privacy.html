1 · Who we are

donna ("donna," "we," "us," or "our") is a personal AI service operated by Arnav Jhajharia, based in Singapore. For the purposes of the Singapore Personal Data Protection Act, 2012 ("PDPA"), the EU/UK General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), the California Consumer Privacy Act as amended by the CPRA ("CCPA"), and the Indian Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 ("DPDP Act"), donna acts as the data controller (or, under the PDPA, the Organisation; under the DPDP Act, the Data Fiduciary) of the personal data described in this policy.

Contact for privacy matters: arnav@itsmedonna.com.

2 · Scope

This policy applies to the donna mobile application (iOS and Android, built with Expo), the donna WhatsApp surface, the donna iMessage surface, and any donna-operated server, API, or backend service that processes data on behalf of an end user (collectively, the "Service").

This policy does not cover third-party services we integrate with (Google Workspace, Notion, Spotify, etc.) — those providers maintain their own privacy policies, which govern the data you store with them. We describe below what we access from those providers and what we do with it.

3 · Data we collect

3.1 Data you give us directly

  • Account identifiers. Apple Sign-In identifier, name (if shared), and email (if shared) via Clerk. WhatsApp phone number (E.164) when you message donna's WhatsApp number.
  • Conversation content. Every message you send to donna across any surface (WhatsApp, iMessage, mobile chat, voice transcripts), and every message donna sends back. This includes audio captured during a voice call, which is transcribed in transit.
  • Triggers and reminders. Anything you ask donna to remember, follow up on, or do later.
  • Support correspondence. Anything you send to our support email.

3.2 Data we collect via OAuth integrations (with your explicit consent)

When you connect a third-party account, we receive an OAuth access token and use it to read data on your behalf. Specifically:

  • Google Workspace — Gmail message content, headers, labels, and metadata (read-only by default; send scopes requested only for tools that send mail); Google Calendar events, attendees, and timing; Google Drive file metadata and, on demand, file content for files you explicitly reference.
  • Notion — page and database content within workspaces you authorize.
  • Spotify — recently-played tracks, playlists, and listening history.
  • Additional integrations may be added over time. New integrations always require a fresh consent step before any data is read.

3.3 Data we collect automatically

  • Device metadata. Device model, OS version, app version, locale, time zone, and a non-reversible install identifier used for push notifications.
  • Push tokens. Apple Push Notification Service (APNs) tokens, including VoIP push tokens, so donna can wake your device.
  • Diagnostic logs. Server-side request logs (timestamps, route paths, status codes, latency). These do not include message bodies in production.
  • Operational telemetry. Latency, error rates, and tool-call traces sent to our observability provider (see sub-processors). Traces may include prompt and response content for the purpose of debugging your account; retention is bounded (see §6).

3.4 Data we do not collect

  • We do not use cookies for tracking or advertising. Authentication uses session tokens, not third-party cookies.
  • We do not collect precise GPS location.
  • We do not buy, rent, or augment your data from data brokers.
  • We do not run advertising of any kind on donna.

5 · Legal bases (GDPR / UK GDPR)

Where the GDPR or UK GDPR applies, we rely on the following legal bases:

  • Contract (Art. 6(1)(b)) — to provide the Service you have signed up for.
  • Consent (Art. 6(1)(a)) — for each third-party OAuth integration, for any optional analytics, and for any processing of special-category data you choose to share with donna in conversation. You may withdraw consent at any time.
  • Legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f)) — for security, fraud prevention, debugging, and core service operation, where this does not override your fundamental rights.
  • Legal obligation (Art. 6(1)(c)) — where applicable law requires us to retain or disclose data.

Under the DPDP Act, processing is conducted on the basis of your consent, or, where applicable, certain legitimate uses recognized by the Act.

7 · Sub-processors and sharing

We use a small set of vetted infrastructure providers to operate the Service. Each is contractually bound to confidentiality and to processing your data only on our documented instructions. The current list is maintained at /subprocessors.

We do not sell, rent, or trade personal data. We do not share personal data with third parties for their own marketing or analytics.

We may disclose personal data:

  • to comply with a valid legal process (subpoena, court order) — we notify you unless legally prohibited;
  • to protect the rights, safety, or property of donna, our users, or the public;
  • in connection with a merger, acquisition, or asset sale — in which case we will notify you at least 30 days in advance, and you may delete your account before the transfer takes effect.

8 · Where data lives

donna is operated from Singapore. Primary data storage (Postgres via Supabase) is hosted in a region selected at provisioning time. Compute for ancillary services (e.g. browser automation via donna-browser) runs on Amazon Web Services in ap-south-1 (Mumbai, India). Some sub-processors operate in the United States and the European Union; see /subprocessors for the location of each.

Cross-border transfers. Where personal data is transferred outside your jurisdiction (e.g., from the EU/UK to the United States, or from Singapore to other regions), we rely on the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses, the UK International Data Transfer Addendum, Singapore PDPA's prescribed transfer mechanisms, or the recipient's certification under an adequacy framework, as appropriate. For DPDP Act purposes, transfers occur only to jurisdictions not restricted by the Central Government under section 16 of the Act.

9 · Google user data — Limited Use

donna's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Specifically, with respect to data obtained from Google APIs (Gmail, Calendar, Drive, and any future Google scope you grant):

  • We do not use it to develop, improve, or train generalized AI/ML models.
  • We do not transfer it to third parties except as necessary to provide or improve the Service for you, comply with applicable law, or as part of a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets with notice to you.
  • We do not use it to serve advertisements.
  • Humans do not read it, except: (a) with your explicit affirmative consent for support; (b) when required by law; (c) when necessary for security investigations (e.g., to investigate abuse); or (d) in aggregated/anonymized form for service performance.

A standalone restatement of this commitment is available at /limited-use-policy.

10 · Your rights

Subject to the law applicable to you, you have the following rights over your personal data:

  • Access — obtain a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correction — correct inaccurate or incomplete personal data.
  • Deletion / erasure — request deletion of your account and associated data.
  • Portability — receive your data in a machine-readable format (JSON).
  • Restriction and objection — restrict or object to certain processing.
  • Withdraw consent — at any time, for any integration or processing relying on consent.
  • CCPA — California residents have the right to know, delete, correct, and opt out of "sale" or "sharing" of personal information. donna does not sell or share personal information as those terms are defined under the CCPA.
  • DPDP Act — Indian residents have the rights to access, correction, completion, updating, and erasure, as well as grievance redressal and nomination, as set out in sections 11–14 of the Act.
  • Complaint — lodge a complaint with your local supervisory authority (in the EU, your national Data Protection Authority; in the UK, the ICO; in India, the Data Protection Board).

To exercise any of these rights, email arnav@itsmedonna.com. We respond within 30 days (45 days under the DPDP Act, with possible extension where the Act permits).

11 · Security

We apply commercially reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your data, including: TLS in transit; encryption at rest for the primary database and backups; OAuth tokens stored in encrypted form; row-level isolation by user identifier; principle of least privilege for engineering access; periodic credential rotation; multi-factor authentication on all administrative accounts.

No method of transmission or storage is perfectly secure. In the event of a data breach affecting your personal data, we will notify you and the relevant authorities as required by applicable law (within 72 hours under the GDPR; without undue delay under the DPDP Act).

12 · Children

donna is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 13. If we learn that we have collected personal data from a child under 13 without verified parental consent, we will delete that data. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, contact arnav@itsmedonna.com. Where local law requires a higher minimum age (e.g., 16 in parts of the EU under GDPR Art. 8), that higher threshold applies for users in those jurisdictions.
