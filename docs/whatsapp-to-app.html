whatsapp to app

how a user who first met donna on whatsapp ends up installing the app. parked here while we focus on app-first onboarding; come back when whatsapp ingress is a real channel.

the premise

we have two surfaces, whatsapp and the app. a user can enter from either. when the user enters via whatsapp, the conversion problem is: how do we get them to install the app without it feeling like a banner ad inside an intimate conversation.

the proposal

make it button-driven. whatsapp supports interactive reply buttons (up to three per message) and url cta buttons. taps replace typing, deep-link straight into the app on install. lower friction, clearer affordance, faster funnel.

what only the app delivers

buttons are mechanics. the conversion is earned by what the app can do that whatsapp cannot.

  • voice calls via callkit — donna rings you, you answer like a real call
  • push notifications you can act on from the lock screen
  • dynamic island — donna lives on the home screen, not in a chat thread
  • history and dashboard — every action donna took, browsable, reversible
  • live mode — a place to enter things in the moment without typing into a chat
  • widgets — donna visible without opening anything

whatsapp is text. the app is presence. the conversion message has to make presence felt.

the shape of the offer

not "install the app." that's a cold ask. the offer is donna noticing a moment where the app would help her help you — "i can ring you about this if you want, want me to?" — and the button is the answer to her question, not a marketing cta. the install happens because the user said yes to donna, not yes to an app.

the button carries the conversion. the offer earns it.

voice register

button labels follow donna's voice. lowercase, no exclamations, conversational. "ring me" not "Call Now". "see it" not "View Dashboard". whatsapp's default button styling already feels corporate; the labels are the only lever we have to keep her tone intact.

open questions

  • which app capability is the strongest single conversion hook — voice, dashboard, widget? probably voice, but unverified.
  • do we deep-link into a specific app screen post-install (the call, the dashboard) or into the normal onboarding?
  • how do we handle android users until the android app exists?
  • do we ever not offer the app — keep some users whatsapp-only because that's their preference?
