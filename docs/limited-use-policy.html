Limited Use Policy

donna's commitment to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements, with respect to data obtained from Google APIs.

Last updated: 2026-05-24  ·  Effective: 2026-05-24  ·  Version 1.0

Affirmation

donna's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Scope

This policy governs all data donna receives from Google APIs — including Gmail, Google Calendar, Google Drive, and any additional Google scope you may grant in the future ("Google User Data"). It applies to every part of donna's infrastructure that touches Google User Data, including our sub-processors listed at /subprocessors.

Specific commitments

1. No training of generalized models

We do not use Google User Data to develop, improve, or train generalized AI/ML models. The model that powers donna (Anthropic Claude) is a third-party model whose training corpus is controlled by its provider; we do not contribute Google User Data to that corpus. We also do not train, fine-tune, or distill any in-house model on Google User Data.

2. No transfer except to operate the Service

We do not transfer Google User Data to third parties except:

  • as necessary to provide or improve user-facing features of the Service for the user from whom the data was obtained (e.g., sending an email body to the language-model provider so donna can summarize it for you);
  • to comply with applicable law or legal process;
  • as part of a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, in which case you will receive notice and the option to delete your account before the transfer takes effect.

The full list of sub-processors that may receive Google User Data is maintained at /subprocessors. Each is contractually restricted to processing Google User Data on donna's documented instructions.

3. No advertising

We do not use Google User Data to serve advertisements. donna does not run advertising of any kind, and Google User Data is never used for ad targeting, retargeting, personalized advertising, or interest-category derivation.

4. No human reading, except as narrowly permitted

Humans do not read Google User Data, except:

  • (a) with your explicit affirmative consent, given through a specific in-product or written request, and only for the support purpose you have consented to;
  • (b) when required by law, in response to valid legal process; we will notify you of the request unless legally prohibited;
  • (c) when necessary for security investigations, including investigating suspected abuse, security incidents, or violations of our Terms; or
  • (d) in aggregated or anonymized form for service-performance monitoring, where the data has been processed so that it cannot reasonably be linked back to an identifiable individual.

How we enforce this in practice

  • Google User Data is segregated per user and stored under a per-user identifier. Engineering access to production data stores is restricted, audited, and granted on a least-privilege basis.
  • OAuth tokens for Google scopes are stored encrypted at rest. Scope grants are time-limited and refreshed only as needed to keep the Service working for you.
  • Caches of fetched Google User Data are bounded by the retention rules in the Privacy Policy and are purged on revocation or account deletion within the periods stated there.
  • Outbound transfers of Google User Data to sub-processors (e.g., inference requests carrying email content) occur over TLS to vetted providers under DPAs that prohibit secondary use.
  • We do not log Google User Data into long-lived debug systems. Where short-lived debug traces contain Google User Data, they are subject to bounded retention and access controls.

Your control

You can revoke donna's access to your Google account at any time, either from within the donna app or from Google Account → Security → Third-party access. Revocation stops new data flow immediately. Cached Google User Data is purged in accordance with the retention schedule in our Privacy Policy.

Contact

Changes

We may update this policy as the Service evolves or as Google's User Data Policy is revised. Material changes will be announced through the Service. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision.
