what this is

the graph i should have drawn first

flows.html shows twenty pages grouped by theme. it doesn't show how a user actually moves between them — and that's where the real design lives. this is the inter-page graph. nodes are pages. edges are transitions. ambient amber edges are the ones i hadn't decided on yet, and drawing this surfaced them.

unresolved · the amber edges

five decisions i couldn't make alone

drawing the DAG surfaced five places where the user flow has an ambiguity, a missing entry, or a rule that contradicts itself. these need a call before we ship pages.

argue these

  1. how do you reach "held back" (page 07)? right now there's no tap path. options: a small "i held three things back from you" footer link on page 05; a permanent fourth card on page 05 (breaks "three things" headline); or only via push when donna's holding-back queue gets large. preferred: the footer link, since it's a soft entry that doesn't break the headline.
  2. the drill-in card (06) lands in three different places. if the card was about a person → memory (12). if it was about an email → inbox draft (18). if it was about a reply you owe → chat with prefill (08). this means the *same screen* (06) has three possible next screens. either the drill-in page needs three different layouts (verbose), or the destination is always chat (loses precision), or we accept that the cta verb on page 06 changes per card type. preferred: the cta verb shifts ("draft a reply" / "open the memory" / "see the inbox").
  3. integrations (14-16) have no entry point. settings root (19) doesn't list integrations. options: add an "integrations" item to settings; promote integrations to a top-level tab (breaks 4-tab discipline); or make it accessible only via push when an integration breaks. preferred: add to settings root, demote from "main flow" status.
  4. inbox copilot (17-18) has no clear entry either. not in tabs, not in settings, not in today's three things by default. options: it's the *content* of "draft a reply" from 06; or it's its own tab (5-tab); or push notifications are its only entry. preferred: fold inbox copilot *into* chat as a special bubble type, deleting it as a separate surface entirely. drops the page count to 18.
  5. push notifications go where? i listed 5 push vectors → 5 destinations. but real push payloads carry varying intent. one rule needs to be picked: do pushes always open chat (donna's voice as the lobby), or do they deep-link to the relevant surface (today, configure, draft)? preferred: chat lobby for trust ("donna sent you something") + a one-tap jump from the bubble to the relevant deep screen. costs an extra tap, gains coherent voice.

the shape of it

what the DAG tells us

  • there are seven independent entry vectors (cold start ×2, push ×4, deep link). only two land on a flow start; five land mid-flow. mid-flow entries need to render cleanly with no back-stack — verify each.
  • page 05 (today) is the most-connected node — five outbound edges. that's correct, but it means 05 carries disproportionate complexity and is the highest-stakes page.
  • page 06 (drill-in) has three diverging cross-flow paths. ambiguity sits there. fix it by typing the card.
  • the chat long-press sheet (10) is a cross-flow router — three of its four actions exit the flow. it might deserve to be its own first-class surface, not a sheet.
  • integrations (14-16) and inbox copilot (17-18) are both orphaned. five pages out of twenty have no clear inbound path. that's a 25% orphan rate.
  • forgetting (13) is the only oxblood edge in the entire DAG. that's a feature — destructive should be rare.
