what changed

three tabs, three questions

v1 had seven flows scattered across four tabs with five orphaned pages. v2 collapses the IA into three tabs, each answering a distinct question about donna. the structure deletes six surfaces by reframing — most of the inbox copilot folds into chat, and the morning brief becomes the first burst of the day rather than its own screen.

record

causes → present → record. dashboard configures, live executes, history remembers. each tab earns its existence by answering a question the others can't.

resolved · what the IA fixed

five amber edges, gone

cleaned

  1. "held back" entry — solved. now a section in H1, reachable by tab + scroll.
  2. drill-in's three exits — solved. L2 stays a sheet inside live; cross-tab links to H2 (memory) are explicit secondary actions, not branching destinations. there's only one "main" path now.
  3. integrations orphan — solved. dashboard's whole job is to expose them. D1 → D2.
  4. inbox copilot orphan — solved by deletion. it's bubble types in L1, not a separate surface.
  5. push destination — solved. all pushes land in L1 with donna's voice carrying the news. cross-tab deep links happen via in-chat ctas ("fix it" → D3), not via the push itself.

still open

the questions the IA created

argue these

  1. what's L1 (live) when nothing's happening? empty composer with a faded "she's quiet right now"? or the most recent burst still sitting there? or always the morning brief, even at 11 pm? my pick: the most recent burst stays visible — donna's voice persists, the composer is always ready.
  2. history as timeline or sectioned? timeline (everything by date, mixed types) is honest about donna's life-of-record. sectioned (memories / past chats / past briefs) is browseable. my pick: default to timeline with type chips on each row, with a filter button to constrain to one type.
  3. are past chats read-only in history? if yes, "resume thread" jumps back to L1 with context. if no, history doubles as a chat archive and the distinction with L1 blurs. my pick: read-only in H, tappable to "resume in live."
  4. is "dashboard" the right name? implies analytics. closer to "controls" or "setup." but "dashboard" reads as the brand-y center of donna's operation. my pick: ship as dashboard for v1, revisit once the actual content tells us what it really is.
  5. does morning brief deserve a distinct visual treatment in L1? if it's just three bubbles, it competes with regular chat. could be a single "brief" bubble that expands. or a date-header treatment ("thursday · brief"). my pick: a serif-italic eyebrow above the day's first burst — "may 21 · brief" — that visually marks the day's opening without becoming a separate surface.
